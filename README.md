  I design and build <b>bare-metal firmware</b> on STM32 microcontrollers —
  configuring GPIO, EXTI, NVIC, and SPI directly at the register level, with
  no HAL or LL abstraction layers in between. My work sits at the
  intersection of hardware and software: signal verification with an
  oscilloscope and logic analyzer, real-time interrupt-driven control, and
  communication protocols such as CAN, UART, SPI, and I2C.

Bare-metal STM32F407 projects covering GPIO, external interrupts (EXTI), RCC
